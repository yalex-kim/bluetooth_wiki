Core Specification Deprecation and Withdrawal Policy
Deprecation:
• The minimum deprecation date of a version of the Bluetooth Core Specification is ten years from the date of adoption.
• Thirty-six months prior to the estimated deprecation date, the Bluetooth Board of Directors will review the planned deprecation date and either confirm it or set a later date.
Withdrawal:
• The minimum withdrawal date of a version of the Bluetooth Core specification is five years from the date of deprecation.
