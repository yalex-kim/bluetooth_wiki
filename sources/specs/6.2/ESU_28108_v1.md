Abstract:
This erratum aligns the in-band spurious emission and interference performance requirements in Volume 2, Part A (BR/EDR Radio Physical Layer Speciﬁcation) with those in Volume 6, Part A (LE Radio Physical Layer Speciﬁcation).

#### 2.1.1 [Modified Section] 3.1.2.1 In-band spurious emission

[The modiﬁed text with changes is shown below.]
Within the ISM band the transmitter shall pass a spectrum mask, given in Table 3.1. The spectrum shall comply with the 20 dB bandwidth deﬁnition in FCC part 15.247 and shall be measured accordingly. In addition to the FCC requirement anAn adjacent channel power on adjacentis speciﬁed for channels with a difference in RF channel number of two or greater is deﬁnedat least 1 MHz from the carrier. This adjacent channel power is deﬁned as the sum of the measured power in a 1 MHz bandwidth. The transmitted powerspectrum measurement shall be measured inperformed with a 100 kHz resolution bandwidth using maximum holdand an average detector. The device shall transmit on the RF channel with the center frequency M and the adjacent channel power shall be measured on RFa 1 MHz channel numbercentered on RF frequency N. The transmitter shall transmit a pseudo -random data pattern in the payload throughout the test.

| Frequency offset | Transmit Spurious Power |
| --- | --- |
| ± 500 kHz | -20 dBc |
| 2 MHz (\|M-N\| = 2) | -20 dBm |
| 3 MHz or greater (\|M-N\| ≥ 3) | -4030 dBm |

Table 3.1: Transmit Spectrum mask
Note: If the output power is less than 0 dBm then, wherever appropriate, the FCC's 20 dB relative requirement overrules the absolute adjacent channel power requirement stated in Table 3.1.
Exceptions are allowed in up to three bands of 1 MHz width centered on a frequency which is an integer multiple of 1 MHz. They shall comply with an absolutehave a value of –20 dBm or less.

#### 2.1.2 [Modified Section] 3.2.2.1 In-band spurious emission

[The modiﬁed text with changes is shown below.]
Within the ISM band the power spectral density of the transmitter shall comply with the following requirements when sending pseudo random data. All power measurements shall use a 100 kHz resolution bandwidth with maximum hold. The power measurements between 1 MHz and 1.5 MHz from the carrier shall be at least 26 dB below the maximum power measurement up to 500 kHz from the carrier an average detector. The adjacent channel power for channels at least 2 MHz from the carrier is deﬁned as the sum of the power measurements over a 1 MHz channel and shall not exceed -20 dBm for the second adjacent channels and -4030 dBm for the third and subsequent adjacent channels. These requirements shall apply to the transmitted signal from the start of the guard time to the end of the power down ramp. The spectral mask is illustrated in Figure 3.2.
Exceptions are allowed in up to 3 bands of 1 MHz width centered on a frequency which is an integer multiple of 1 MHz. They shall comply with an absolutehave a value of –20 dBm or less.
[Delete Figure 3.2.]
